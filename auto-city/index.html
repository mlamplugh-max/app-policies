Auto City Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Published by MWL Applications

Auto City is a garage-building and car-collecting game for Android and the web. This policy explains what the app does with information, which is very little.

What we collect

Nothing. Auto City does not collect, store or transmit personal information. The game has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases in this version. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, cars, garage layout, settings and the garage name you choose) is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage or your browser's storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it. The Settings screen lets you export your save as a file and import it again; that file goes only where you put it.

Photo mode

Photo mode draws a picture of your garage and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that picture goes is entirely your choice; the app keeps no copy.

Children

Auto City is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example optional purchases or an online feature), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the app store listing will say so.
