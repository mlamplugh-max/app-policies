Auto City Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Published by MWL Applications

Auto City is a garage-building and car-collecting game for Android and the web. Version 1.1 adds optional online drag racing. This policy explains what the game does with information in both parts. In short: the garage game collects nothing, and online racing collects only what is needed to run races, and only after you choose to turn it on.

The garage game (always offline)

Your game progress (coins, cars, garage layout, settings and the garage name you choose) is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage or your browser's storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it. The Settings screen lets you export your save as a file and import it again; that file goes only where you put it. If you have device backup turned on, Android may include the game's saved data in your Google backup; that is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us. Photo mode draws a picture of your garage and hands it to your device's share sheet; where it goes is your choice, and the app keeps no copy. Practice runs in the Race tab are also offline and send nothing.

Online racing (optional, off until you turn it on)

Quick Races, Duels and the Daily Drag Cup need our race server. Nothing is sent to it until you tap Go online in the Race tab. If you never do, the app never contacts the server.

Once you go online, this is what the server receives and keeps:

A random driver IDCreated by our server for you. It identifies your driver, not you.
A random install code, stored only as a one-way hashThe app makes a random code the first time you go online. The server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy, so it can recognise your phone as the same driver next time. It is not your Android ID, advertising ID, phone number or any hardware identifier.
A made-up driver nameFor example “Bold Otter 221”. The server generates it. You cannot type a name, so no personal name or free text is collected.
Your race activityThe car you race with, the wager you choose, the timing of your taps (your reaction and your two gear shifts), the result of each race, coin payouts and refunds the server owes you, and your Duel and Daily Drag Cup entries. A simple count of your races, wins and losses.
Rival difficulty and prize countsA number that adjusts how hard the computer-controlled Rival is, based on your own recent results, and a count of the daily prize purses you have collected today.
DatesWhen your driver was created, when it last connected to the race server, and a time stamp on each race, challenge and cup entry.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, advertising ID or any payment information. There is no chat and no way to type anything to another player.

What other players see

Another player can see your made-up driver name, the car you chose and your wager when you post or take a Duel, and your result and run (reaction time, run time and shift quality) when you race them. Any driver who is online can see the Daily Drag Cup bracket, including the made-up names of everyone entered. Computer-controlled opponents are always labelled “Rival”.

Why we use it

To run races and keep them fair: signing your driver back in, replaying your taps on the server to check every result, paying and refunding coins, running the daily cup, and limiting abuse. We do not use it for advertising or analytics, and we do not sell it. The only adaptation is that the computer-controlled Rival’s difficulty follows your own recent results. There are no ads, trackers or analytics tools in the game.

Who holds it

Our race server and its database are hosted by Railway (railway.com) in the United States. Railway stores and processes the data on our behalf. Like any host, it sees the network address of each connection in order to deliver it. Our server uses that address only in memory, briefly, to keep sign-ups and requests within sensible limits, and does not write it to our database or to our own server logs. Railway may keep ordinary connection logs of its own. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We do not share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

Coins have no real-world value

Coins in Auto City are play money. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out in this version, and wagers in races use coins only.

Deleting your data and how long we keep it

In the Race tab, tap Delete my racing data and confirm. This removes your driver name and your driver’s link to your phone from our server, withdraws any open challenges, pays you any wagers or winnings still waiting (a cup entry fee is not returned once that cup’s registration has closed), clears the sign-in from your phone and turns online racing off again. Your garage and coins stay on your phone.

What remains is a record with a generic name (“Former driver”) and no connection to you or your device. Race results you took part in (the timings, the car and the time) stay attached to that record so other players’ race history still makes sense, and in your opponents’ messages about those races your old name is replaced by “Former driver”. Delivery notes about payouts are removed about a month after your phone has collected them.

If you do not delete it yourself, we remove the link to your device and your name automatically once your driver has not connected to the race server for 12 months. If you uninstall the app the server cannot tell, so please delete before you uninstall; otherwise the record is removed after those 12 months. Records of drivers removed for abuse may be kept longer.

We cannot look up a person by name or email, because we do not hold either, and driver names are public. If you cannot use the button, email us the Driver ID shown under “Your online racing data” in the Race tab (we need the ID, not the name) and we will delete that driver.

Children

Auto City is not directed at children under 13 and online racing is meant for people 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used online racing, email us and we will delete the record.

Your choices

You can use the whole garage game without ever going online. You can delete your racing data at any time, as above. Depending on where you live you may have further rights over data about you; because we hold only a random ID, we can usually act on a request only for a driver whose ID you can give us, and we will tell you if we cannot.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected (for example optional purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the store listing will say so.
