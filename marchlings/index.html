Marchlings Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026. Published by Mark W. Lamplugh Jr.

Summary

Marchlings is a puzzle game that runs on your device and does not collect or send any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. Marchlings has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software that collects data. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level progress and settings in the app's own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not receive any.

Children

Marchlings does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
