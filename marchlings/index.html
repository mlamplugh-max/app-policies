Marchlings Privacy Policy

Updated 22 November 2026, for version 2.1. Published by Mark W. Lamplugh Jr.

Summary

Marchlings is a puzzle game that runs on your device. We do not collect or send any personal data. From version 2.1 the Android app also shows ads from Google AdMob; what Google's advertising software collects, directly from your phone, is described under Ads.

What the app collects

Nothing, by us. Marchlings has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of ours that it talks to. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The only software in it that collects anything is Google's advertising software in the Android app, from version 2.1: see Ads.

What stays on your device

The game saves your level progress and settings in the app's own storage on your device. From version 2.1 that also records which levels you opened by watching an ad and how many you opened today. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Android app, from version 2.1)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then after a level you have won (never during play), and an ad you can choose to watch to open the next level after one you could not finish. Version 2.0 and earlier, and the web version, show no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software in the app collects and uses, directly from your phone: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your network (IP) address, from which a general location can be estimated, information about how you interact with the app and with ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent over an encrypted connection.

In countries where the law requires it, Google asks for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in the game, on the title screen or on the pause card, under "Ad privacy choices". Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads). How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

A level opened by watching an ad is recorded on your phone only, like the rest of your progress. We keep no record of the ads you watch.

Marchlings is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to those Google rates as suitable for general audiences.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not receive any. The information Google's advertising service collects goes straight from your phone to Google; it does not pass through any server of ours and we cannot see it.

Children

Marchlings is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. We receive none from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
