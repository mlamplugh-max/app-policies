Prism Run Privacy Policy

Updated 22 November 2026. Published by Mark W. Lamplugh Jr.

Summary

Prism Run is a game that runs on your device. It has no account, and we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information ourselves. From version 0.6.0 the Android app also shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google’s advertising software collects some information directly from your phone; that is described under “Ads” below. We do not receive any of it. Versions before 0.6.0 show no ads and contain no advertising software.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. Prism Run has no accounts, no analytics of our own and no server of our own that the app talks to. We, the developer, never receive any information about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings, progress and best-run ghosts in the app’s own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Motion sensors

If you turn on tilt steering in Settings, the game reads your device’s motion sensor to steer the marble while you play. The live readings are used at that moment and are not stored or sent anywhere. If you calibrate tilt, the neutral position you set is saved in the game’s settings on your phone, like your other settings.

Ads (Android app, from version 0.6.0)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then after you finish a course (never during a run, and not until you have finished a few courses), and an ad you can choose to watch to retry a Campaign course you ran out of time on with extra time, up to three times a day. Nothing in the game is for sale.

To show and measure ads, Google’s advertising software in the app collects and uses, directly from your phone: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, your network (IP) address, from which a general location can be estimated, information about how you interact with the app and with ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent over an encrypted connection.

In countries where the law requires it, Google asks for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in the game under Settings, Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads). How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The extra time you earn by watching an ad is applied on your phone for that one attempt. Nothing about the ads you watch is sent to us; the game only keeps, on your phone, how many of those retries you have used today.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data ourselves, because we do not receive any. The information Google’s advertising service collects goes straight from your phone to Google, under Google’s own policies; it does not pass through us and we cannot see it.

Children

Prism Run is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not collect personal information from anyone, and the ads Google shows in the game are limited to those it rates as suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
