Porcupine Panic Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Updated November 22, 2026. Published by Mark W. Lamplugh Jr.

Summary

Porcupine Panic is a game that runs on your device. The game itself has no accounts and does not collect or send any personal data to us. From version 0.4.0 the Android app also shows ads from Google AdMob; what Google’s advertising software collects is described under Ads below.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Porcupine Panic has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Apart from Google’s advertising software (see Ads), it contains no third-party software that collects data.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings, unlocked levels, best scores, coins and the number of bonus ads you have watched today in the app’s own storage on your device. This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Android app, from version 0.4.0)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then when you leave a level’s result screen (never during a level), and an ad you can choose to watch for bonus coins.

To show and measure ads, Google’s advertising software in the app collects and uses, directly from your phone: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID; your network (IP) address, from which a general location can be estimated; information about how you interact with the app and with ads (such as app launches, taps and video views); and diagnostic information about how the app and the ads perform. Google uses it for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is sent over an encrypted connection, straight from your phone to Google: it does not pass through us and we cannot see it.

In countries where the law requires it, Google asks for your choice about personalized ads before any ad is requested, and you can change that choice at any time in the game, on the title screen, under Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (under Google, Ads, or under Privacy, Ads). How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The bonus coins you earn by watching an ad are added on your phone, like every other coin in the game. We keep no record of the ads you watch.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not receive any. The information Google’s advertising software collects goes straight from your phone to Google.

Children

Porcupine Panic is meant for players aged 13 and over. We do not collect personal information from anyone. The ads in the Android app are limited to those Google rates as suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
