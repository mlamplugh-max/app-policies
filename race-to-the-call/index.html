Race to the Call Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026. Published by MWL Applications (Mark W. Lamplugh Jr.).

Summary

Race to the Call is a first-responder driving game that runs entirely on your device and does not collect or send any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. Race to the Call has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party software that collects data. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (quality level and tilt steering) in the app's own storage on your device. If you turn tilt steering on, the game reads the phone's motion sensor to steer while you play; that reading is used on the spot and never stored or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes the saved settings.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we do not receive any.

Children

Race to the Call does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
